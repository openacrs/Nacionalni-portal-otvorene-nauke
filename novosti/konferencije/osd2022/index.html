Open Science Days IV

 

Belgrade, 3-4 November 2022

 

University of Belgrade organizes Open Science Days IV with the aim of informing the local research community about Open Science principles and current international activities in this area. This year's edition is collocated with the NI4OS-Europe, EOSC Future and WBC-RRI.net dissemination events.
